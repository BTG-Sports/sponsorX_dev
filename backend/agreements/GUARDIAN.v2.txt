SponsorX Guardian Agreement — version 2, effective 22 October 2026
This agreement is between BTG Sports Group LLC, which runs SponsorX ("BTG"), and you, the parent, legal guardian or authorised representative ("Guardian") of the athlete named on this page ("Athlete"), who is under the age of majority where they live.
1. Your authority. You confirm that you are the Athlete's parent, legal guardian or authorised representative, that you have legal authority to make agreements on the Athlete's behalf, and that the identity and guardianship documents you provide are genuine. You will tell BTG at once if your authority ends or changes.
2. Your consent. You consent to the Athlete joining SponsorX, taking part in the sponsorship campaigns you approve, and BTG showing the Athlete's public profile to sponsors for that purpose. You have read the SponsorX Athlete Content Collaboration Agreement and accept it on the Athlete's behalf.
3. You approve before anything happens. Every agreement and every payment for the Athlete is made by you, from your own SponsorX login, before it takes effect. This includes accepting campaign offers and Campaign Orders, listing in the marketplace, setting up the payout account and requesting payouts. The Athlete cannot do these things alone.
4. The Athlete's content. The Athlete may upload their own content. You are emailed each time, and you may ask BTG to take any of it down. All content involving the Athlete is reviewed by a person at BTG before it is published.
5. The Athlete's own rules. The Athlete's school, league, athletic association and state or country may have rules about paid sponsorships. BTG does not check them for each athlete. You confirm that you have checked them, that the Athlete is allowed to take the deals you approve, and that you and the Athlete will follow them, including telling the school about a deal when its rules require it. Following those rules is your and the Athlete's responsibility, not BTG's. If anyone raises a concern about a deal, BTG may pause it and ask you for documents, such as the school's written approval; while it is paused nothing on it moves and its payment is held, and BTG then resumes or cancels it and tells you why.
6. Money and tax. Money the Athlete earns through the marketplace is paid to the payout account you set up with BTG's payment provider, in your name. You agree to hold and use it for the Athlete's benefit. The money is the Athlete's income, so when you set up the payout account, the payment provider asks for the Athlete's tax information (Form W-9), and any tax form BTG must issue is in the Athlete's name.
7. Your information. BTG keeps your name, email, phone number, relationship to the Athlete, government ID and proof of guardianship to verify your authority and to run the account, and any documents you send about a concern under Section 5. Sponsors never see them. Tax information goes to the payment provider, never to SponsorX. Identity files are deleted 30 days after the account closes. BTG keeps the record of each acceptance for 7 years after the account closes.
8. Withdrawing your consent. You may withdraw your consent at any time by telling BTG in writing or closing the account. Campaigns already accepted are completed or cancelled under their own terms, and money already earned is still paid.
9. When the Athlete comes of age. When the Athlete reaches the age of majority, they have 90 days to verify their own identity and take over their account, and this agreement ends for new activity. During those 90 days nothing new can be started. If the Athlete does not verify in time, both accounts are closed, and money already earned remains owed.
10. Accepting online. You accept this agreement by ticking the boxes on this page. That has the same effect as a handwritten signature. SponsorX records the version you accepted, the date and time, your device's internet address and browser, and a fingerprint of the exact text shown. If BTG issues a new version, it applies only after you accept it.
