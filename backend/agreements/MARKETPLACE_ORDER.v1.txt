SPONSORX MARKETPLACE ORDER — STANDARD TERMS, VERSION 1

1. What this is.
This order is between the sponsor named at checkout ("you") and BTG Sports
Group ("BTG"), which runs the SponsorX marketplace on behalf of the teams,
schools and athletes who list on it. The order's own details — the items, the
quantities, the dates, the prices, the fees and the total — are shown with
this document and are fixed on the order when you place it.

2. BTG's approval.
Some orders are reviewed by BTG before they are confirmed: large orders, a
sponsor's first order, and items whose seller asks for review. The checkout
and the order page say when this applies and why. While BTG reviews the order
the items stay held for you; if BTG declines it, the order is cancelled, the
items go back on sale and nothing is charged.

3. Payment.
You pay the full total by card once the order is confirmed. Payment is taken
by the marketplace's payment provider on its own page; SponsorX never asks for
or stores card or bank numbers. Nothing is charged when you place the order.

4. Cancelling.
You may cancel the order at any time before it is paid. After payment, a
cancellation is a refund and is handled by BTG.

5. Delivery and confirmation.
Each seller marks its items delivered. You have 24 hours from that moment to
confirm delivery or raise a problem; if you do not answer, the item counts as
confirmed. The order closes 30 days after confirmation.

6. Use of what you buy.
You may use the items you buy only as each listing describes, for the dates
shown. Any other use needs a new agreement.

7. Billing contact.
Invoices and receipts for this order go to the billing contact you confirm at
checkout. Keep it current with BTG.

8. Your acceptance.
By accepting, you confirm you are authorised to place this order on the
sponsor's behalf, and that you have read this document and the order details
shown with it. Your acceptance records this version, the time, and a
fingerprint of exactly this text, so that what you agreed to can always be
shown.
