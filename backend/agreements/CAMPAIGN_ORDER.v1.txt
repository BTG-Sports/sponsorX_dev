SPONSORX CAMPAIGN ORDER — STANDARD TERMS, VERSION 1

1. What this is.
This Campaign Order is between you (the athlete) and BTG Sports Group ("BTG"),
which manages the campaign on the sponsor's behalf. The order's own details —
the sponsor, the job, your compensation, the usage rights, any exclusivity and
the due date — are shown with this document and are fixed on the order when
BTG sends it. They do not change unless BTG issues a new order.

2. What you agree to deliver.
You agree to create and publish the deliverables listed for this job, by the
due dates shown, following the sponsor's brief and the SponsorX content
guidelines. BTG reviews each draft before it is published and may ask for
revisions. You publish only content BTG has approved.

3. Disclosure.
Every sponsored post must clearly disclose the paid relationship, using the
disclosure BTG specifies for the platform. You will not remove or obscure it.

4. Usage rights.
The sponsor may use the content you create for this order only as the usage
rights on this order describe, and only for the period they state. Any other
use needs a new agreement with you.

5. Exclusivity.
If this order lists exclusivity, you agree not to promote a competing brand in
the stated category for the stated period. If it lists none, you are free to
work with other brands.

6. Compensation.
BTG pays the compensation shown on this order after your deliverables are
approved and published and the sponsor's payment has cleared. SponsorX shows
the status of your earning; payment itself is made outside SponsorX. BTG never
asks for your bank details or tax ID inside SponsorX.

7. Minors.
If you are under 18, this order can only be accepted once your parent or legal
guardian has been verified by BTG, and your guardian's authorization is recorded
with your acceptance.

8. Cancelling.
Either you or BTG may cancel an order before any deliverable is published by
telling the other in writing. Once content is published, the terms above apply
to that content.

9. Your acceptance.
By accepting, you confirm you have read this document and the order details
shown with it. Your acceptance records this version, the time, and a
fingerprint of exactly this text, so that what you agreed to can always be
shown.
