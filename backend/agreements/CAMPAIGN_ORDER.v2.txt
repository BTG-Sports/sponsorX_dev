SPONSORX CAMPAIGN ORDER — STANDARD TERMS, VERSION 2

BTG Sports Group LLC · Effective 22 October 2026

Every Campaign Order has two parts. The order details (the sponsor and campaign, the job, the deliverables and their due dates, the campaign window, your compensation, the usage rights, any paid-media rights, any exclusivity, the disclosures you must make, the tags, links and codes, and whether the sponsor reviews) are shown with these terms and are fixed when BTG sends the order. These standard terms are the same for every order.

1. Who this order is between

This Campaign Order is between you, the athlete named on it, and BTG Sports Group LLC ("BTG"), which manages the campaign on the sponsor's behalf. It is made under the SponsorX Athlete Content Collaboration Agreement you have accepted. If the two conflict, this order governs for this campaign.

2. The details are fixed

The order details are fixed when BTG sends the order and do not change. If anything needs to change, BTG sends a new order. If you decline an order, it cannot be reopened; BTG may send a new one.

3. Making and submitting the content

You create the deliverables listed in the order details, following the brief, and submit each one through SponsorX by its due date. If you cannot meet a due date, tell BTG through SponsorX as soon as you know.

4. Review and revisions

BTG reviews each draft before it is published. Where the order details say the sponsor reviews too, the sponsor reviews after BTG. Either reviewer may approve the draft or ask for a revision; every revision request states its reason. You make reasonable revisions to bring the content in line with the brief. You publish only content that has been approved.

BTG may let an adult athlete with a clean record of approved content skip BTG's review. Content involving a minor, or a sensitive category (alcohol, tobacco or vaping, cannabis, gambling), is always reviewed by a person at BTG.

5. Publishing and verification

Once approved, you publish the content on the platform and within the window stated, and record the link in SponsorX. BTG then checks that the post is live and matches the approved version ("verified"). You keep the post live for the campaign window and do not edit it in a way that changes the approved content.

6. Disclosure

You disclose the paid partnership in every deliverable, using the disclosure in the order details and any disclosure tool the platform provides, in the way the law (including the FTC Endorsement Guides) and the platform require. You do not hide, shorten or remove the disclosure.

7. Usage rights

The sponsor, BTG and SponsorX may use the approved content only as the usage rights in the order details describe. Paid advertising, boosting, use beyond the stated period, merchandise, AI training or synthetic recreation, and sublicensing are not included unless the order details say so. Any other use needs a new agreement with you.

8. Exclusivity

If the order details state exclusivity, you agree not to promote a competing brand in the stated category for the stated number of days from acceptance. If they say "None", there is no exclusivity.

9. Honest results

You do not buy or artificially generate views, followers, likes, clicks, comments, claims or redemptions for this campaign. Figures you report yourself are shown to the sponsor labelled as self-reported.

10. Your compensation

Your compensation is the amount in the order details. It becomes payable when every deliverable on this order has been verified by BTG and the sponsor's payment for this order has been received. Your compensation is not reduced by BTG's fee.

- Managed campaigns: BTG pays you outside SponsorX within 30 days after the compensation becomes payable. SponsorX shows the status of your earning.
- Where payment is made through SponsorX's payment provider: payment is made to your payout account (for a minor, the guardian's payout account) under the SponsorX Marketplace and Payout Terms.

11. Cancelling

You may decline an order before you accept it. After acceptance, either you or BTG may cancel the order before any deliverable is published by telling the other through SponsorX or in writing. Once a deliverable is published, the order can be cancelled only by agreement.

Because an order can be cancelled only before anything on it is published, no compensation is due for a cancelled order, whoever cancels it.

12. Athletes under the age of majority

If you are under the age of majority in your state or country, this order can be accepted only by your verified parent, guardian or authorised representative, from their own SponsorX login, and compensation is paid as the SponsorX Guardian Agreement describes.

13. Acceptance

Pressing "Accept this order" accepts these standard terms and the order details shown with them. It has the same effect as a handwritten signature. SponsorX records the version of these terms, the date and time, and a fingerprint of the exact text shown.
