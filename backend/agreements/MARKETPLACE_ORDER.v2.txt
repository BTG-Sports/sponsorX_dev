BTG Sports Group LLC · Effective 22 October 2026

The order's own details — the items, the quantities, the dates, the prices, the fees and the total — are shown with these terms and are fixed on the order when you place it.

B1. Who the order is with

Your order is with BTG Sports Group LLC ("BTG"), which runs the SponsorX marketplace and sells on behalf of the teams, schools, venues and athletes who list items ("Sellers"). The person placing the order confirms they have authority to bind the sponsor.

B2. Approval

Some orders need approval before you pay: by the Seller (who has 48 hours to answer, or the order is cancelled), and by BTG for orders above your spending limit. Your spending limit starts at $5,000 and grows with your completed orders, up to $25,000.

B3. Paying

You pay by card on our payment provider's secure page once the order is confirmed, or by another method BTG agrees (bank transfer, cheque or invoice). We remind you on days 1 and 2; an order not paid within 3 days is cancelled. Any buyer fee is shown before you pay. SponsorX never asks for or stores card or bank numbers.

B4. Cancelling

You can cancel any time before you pay. After payment, you can cancel a line free until 3 days before its first date; after that, the Seller has 72 hours to agree. A cancellation after payment is refunded in full for the line.

B5. Delivery and confirming

When a Seller records delivery, you have 24 hours to confirm it or raise a problem. If you do not answer, the item counts as confirmed. The order closes 30 days after confirmation.

B6. Problems and refunds

If you raise a problem, the Seller has 72 hours to answer, and may redeliver (within 90 days) or agree a refund. If you cannot agree, BTG decides. Refunds cover the whole line; partial refunds are not offered. A refund is made to your original payment method.

B7. Rewards you offer to fans

A campaign may include a reward for fans, such as a discount or a free item, which fans claim through a SponsorX QR code and redeem at your booth or store. The reward is yours, and so is the responsibility for it. You confirm that each reward you offer:

- is lawful wherever it is offered and redeemed, including any age limit, and any rules for alcohol, tobacco, cannabis or betting promotions that apply to you;
- is described accurately in its offer text and terms, and you will honour it for every fan who claims it within its limits; and
- is not a prize won by chance and does not require a purchase or payment to claim. SponsorX runs instant rewards only.

Where a reward is limited to fans 18 or over, 21 or over, or ticket holders, your staff check that at redemption. BTG may pause or end a reward at any time, including when anyone raises a concern about it, and will tell you why.

B8. Using what you bought

You may use what you buy only as each listing describes. Any other use needs a separate agreement.

B9. Acceptance

Placing the order accepts these terms. It has the same effect as a handwritten signature. SponsorX records the version, the time and a fingerprint of the exact text shown.
