SPONSORX CONTENT COLLABORATION AGREEMENT — VERSION 1

1. What this covers.
This agreement governs content you create for a brand through SponsorX. A
separate brief is issued for each campaign and sits on top of these terms.

2. Your content.
You own what you make. You grant the brand a licence to use the agreed content
on the channels named in the campaign brief, for the period named there.

3. Disclosure.
You will disclose paid partnerships as required by law and by the platform you
post on. SponsorX will tell you what is required for each campaign.

4. Eligibility.
You confirm the details in this application are true, and that if you are
under the age of majority where you live, your guardian's confirmation is
required before you accept a campaign.

5. Ending it.
Either side can end this agreement in writing. Campaigns already accepted are
completed under their own briefs.
