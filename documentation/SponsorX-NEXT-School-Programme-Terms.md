## What the school signs

This is **one document**, the NEXT Programme Agreement. It is signed by the
school's principal (or their delegate) and names one **faculty advisor**. The
advisor is the school's contact and gets the school's login to SponsorX.
Signing it is all a school needs to do to start an edition.

## The terms

1. **Masthead.** The school owns its publication's name and masthead. SponsorX
   gets a licence to use them for the programme while the agreement runs. The
   regional DMV edition's masthead belongs to SponsorX.

2. **Editorial approval: the faculty advisor.** The advisor approves all
   school-specific content, and every student's participation, before it is
   published. Nothing about the school or its students goes out without the
   advisor's approval.

3. **Production and publishing: SponsorX.** SponsorX decides whether an
   edition goes to production, and in what format. It carries the cost and
   the rights (spec §5.2). An edition only goes ahead when its content is
   ready, its rights are cleared, and it has met its minimum revenue. The
   first edition is a **free digital edition**; print is optional.

4. **Advertising.** SponsorX sells the ads at the rate card in
   `SponsorX-NEXT-Rate-Card-Decision.md`. The school may turn down any
   advertiser in a category its own rules prohibit, before the sale closes.

5. **Unsold ads.** Unsold ad space is filled with editorial content or school
   announcements. The school is never charged for it, and it doesn't carry
   over to the next edition.

6. **Revenue.** Each edition's revenue is split 40% SponsorX, 30% the school's
   programme, 20% student pool and 10% editorial fund. The school's share is
   paid to the programme, never to a person. The student pool funds
   recognition points; **no student is paid directly**.

7. **Student work.** Students keep the copyright in what they create. Each
   contributing student, and a parent or guardian for a minor, grants SponsorX
   and the school a non-exclusive licence to publish that work in the edition
   and its digital archive. Any other commercial reuse needs separate, written
   consent.

8. **Student data.** SponsorX collects only what the programme needs.
   Students' data is never sold, and no student under 18 appears publicly
   without that parent or guardian consent.

9. **Term and exit.** The agreement covers one school year and renews by
   agreement. Either side may end it with 30 days' notice. An edition already
   selling is either completed or cancelled with its advertisers refunded.

## After signing

A BTG admin creates the school as a Property of kind SCHOOL and gives the
named advisor a login as its property manager (`P9-OPS-01`). The pilot
school's simulated record is Northside High School, Bowie MD.
