SponsorX — Phase 1 Wireframes

§38 deliverable · task P1-PMO-01 · v1.0 · 2026-09-12 · companion to SponsorX-Design-Record.md and the screen captures in this folder

Schematic layout record of the twelve §9 core screens as built (branch P1-FE-QA-PMO, fixture stage). Solid-border buttons are live affordances; dashed buttons are deliberately unwired in Stage 1 and carry explanatory tooltips in the build. Portal screens share one chrome: fixed sidebar (logo, nav, role chip), sticky top bar (menu/drawer on phones, theme toggle, notifications, user menu with logout). Every stat carries a provenance chip (§22). Wireframes are desktop-proportioned; all screens reflow to a single column at 360px with the sidebar behind a drawer (see the P1-QA-03 audit).
