## 1. PURPOSE
SponsorX is an athlete content and brand activation network operated by BTG Sports Group LLC.
BTG may from time to time present Athlete with opportunities to participate in paid or sponsored marketing campaigns, social-media content, appearances, product experiences, community activations, content-production sessions, ambassador programs, or other promotional activities (“SponsorX Opportunities”).
Unless otherwise stated in a separate written agreement, Athlete is not obligated to accept any SponsorX Opportunity.

## 2. NON-EXCLUSIVE RELATIONSHIP
This Agreement is intended to establish Athlete as a participant/content collaborator within the SponsorX Athlete Network.
Unless the Parties have executed a separate athlete representation, NIL management, marketing, exclusivity, or similar agreement:
this Agreement does not appoint BTG as Athlete’s exclusive sports representative;
this Agreement does not require Athlete to accept every SponsorX Opportunity;
Athlete may engage in other lawful sponsorship, marketing, NIL, employment, or business activities, subject to any existing contractual obligations and campaign-specific restrictions; and
BTG is not obligated to provide Athlete with any minimum number or value of SponsorX Opportunities.

## 3. CAMPAIGN ORDERS
Each SponsorX Opportunity accepted by Athlete shall be documented through a separate written or electronically accepted SponsorX Campaign Order.
The Campaign Order should identify, as applicable:
Sponsor/brand;
Campaign name;
Required deliverables;
Content format;
Due dates;
Publication dates or windows;
Athlete compensation;
Product or other non-cash consideration;
Content approval requirements;
Usage rights;
Length of usage rights;
Paid advertising rights, if any;
Exclusivity or competitive restrictions;
Required tags, links, promotional codes, or QR codes;
Required advertising/sponsorship disclosures;
Appearance requirements;
Tracking/reporting requirements; and
Payment terms.

## 4. ATHLETE COMPENSATION
Athlete shall receive the compensation stated in each accepted Campaign Order.
Compensation may include cash compensation, products, services, travel reimbursement, incentives, performance bonuses, or other consideration specifically identified in the Campaign Order.
Payment shall be made according to the payment schedule contained in the applicable Campaign Order and subject to Athlete satisfactorily completing the required deliverables.
BTG shall not reduce agreed Athlete compensation except as expressly permitted by the applicable Campaign Order or another signed agreement.
Athlete is responsible for providing any reasonably required payment and tax documentation.

## 5. CONTENT OWNERSHIP
Unless otherwise expressly agreed in writing, Athlete retains ownership of original content created by Athlete.
Athlete grants BTG, SponsorX, and the applicable Sponsor only those licenses and usage rights specifically identified in the Campaign Order and/or Media & Content Release applicable to the campaign.
No perpetual transfer of Athlete’s content rights is created merely by participating in SponsorX.

## 6. ATHLETE NAME, IMAGE & LIKENESS
Campaign-specific use of Athlete’s:
name;
image;
likeness;
voice;
biography;
social-media handle;
photographs;
video;
audio;
approved quotes; and
approved campaign content
shall be governed by the applicable Campaign Order and Athlete Media & Content Release.

## 7. PAID MEDIA & EXTENDED USAGE
A Sponsor’s right to repost approved content organically does not automatically include the right to use Athlete’s content or likeness in paid advertising.
Unless expressly stated in the Campaign Order, the following are not automatically included:
Paid social advertising;
Boosted posts;
Digital advertisements;
Television advertising;
Billboards;
Print advertising;
Perpetual use;
Merchandise;
Product packaging;
Artificial intelligence training or synthetic recreation of Athlete;
Material alteration of Athlete’s voice or likeness; or
Sublicensing to unrelated third parties.
Any such rights should be separately identified and compensated where applicable.

## 8. ATHLETE RESPONSIBILITIES
Athlete agrees to:
provide accurate information to BTG;
perform accepted campaign obligations professionally;
meet agreed deadlines;
promptly communicate circumstances that may prevent timely performance;
follow approved campaign briefs;
make required sponsorship/advertising disclosures;
refrain from purchasing or artificially generating followers, views, likes, clicks, comments, claims, redemptions, or other campaign metrics;
refrain from making false, misleading, medical, nutritional, performance, or product claims not authorized by the campaign;
disclose existing sponsorships, endorsement agreements, NIL agreements, category restrictions, or other conflicts that may affect a SponsorX Opportunity; and
comply with applicable laws, regulations, school/team policies, association requirements, and other rules applicable to Athlete.

## 9. SPONSOR CONFLICTS & EXCLUSIVITY
Athlete shall disclose known sponsorship conflicts before accepting a Campaign Order.
Any campaign-specific exclusivity must be stated in the Campaign Order and should identify:
Restricted Category: [CHANGE PER CAMPAIGN]
Exclusivity Period: [CHANGE PER CAMPAIGN]
Geographic Scope, if applicable: [CHANGE PER CAMPAIGN]
No general category exclusivity is created under this Agreement unless separately stated in writing.

## 10. CONTENT REVIEW & APPROVAL
When a Campaign Order requires pre-publication approval, Athlete shall submit the content to BTG before publishing.
BTG and/or Sponsor may request reasonable revisions necessary to bring content into compliance with the approved campaign brief.
Athlete shall not publish content requiring approval until approval has been received.

## 11. CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCE INFORMATION
Athlete agrees to provide reasonably requested campaign performance information available to Athlete, which may include:
Views;
Reach;
Impressions;
Engagement;
Clicks;
Story analytics;
Promotional-code activity; and
Other campaign-specific metrics.
Athlete authorizes BTG to include campaign performance information in SponsorX reporting and analytics.

## 12. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
Athlete participates as an independent contractor/content collaborator unless another written agreement establishes a different relationship.
Nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee relationship, partnership, joint venture, or franchise relationship between Athlete and BTG.
Athlete is responsible for Athlete’s own taxes and obligations arising from compensation received, subject to applicable law.

## 13. TERM & TERMINATION
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either Party upon written notice.
Termination does not automatically cancel:
Campaign Orders already accepted;
Payment obligations already earned;
Content usage rights previously granted for an agreed period;
Confidentiality obligations; or
Other provisions that by their nature survive termination.
The Parties may mutually agree to cancel or modify an outstanding Campaign Order in writing.

## 14. NO GUARANTEE OF OPPORTUNITIES
BTG does not guarantee Athlete:
Any minimum number of campaigns;
Any minimum amount of compensation;
Any sponsorship;
Any endorsement;
Any increase in followers;
Any athletic opportunity; or
Any professional representation opportunity
solely because Athlete joins the SponsorX Athlete Network.
