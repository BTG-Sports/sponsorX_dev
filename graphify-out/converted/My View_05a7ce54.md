# 7. CPM pricing engine
Don't establish one universal CPM.
The platform should calculate a BTG Media Rate.
Athletiverse currently illustrates its model with a fixed $14 CPM and 500,000 guaranteed views, resulting in a $7,000 spend, while noting that rates vary by property/category. (Athletiverse)
BTG should similarly price dynamically.
### Starting framework
These would be BTG's initial rate-card assumptions, not industry-standard rates. After 6–12 months of performance data, the platform should replace assumptions with observed pricing.
That's consistent with the broader sponsorship industry's move toward audience, exposure and ROI-based valuation rather than simply selling logo placement. Nielsen, for example, evaluates sponsorship through exposure, audience metrics, media rates and sales/brand impact. (Nielsen)

# 8. Guaranteed View Model
This is one of the most important components.
Instead of saying:
"$10,000 gets your logo on our content."
BTG says:
"$10,000 guarantees 600,000 qualified content views."
Now sponsors can compare BTG to advertising.
If the campaign under-delivers:
BTG provides make-good inventory until the guaranteed view floor is reached.
That reduces perceived sponsor risk.
