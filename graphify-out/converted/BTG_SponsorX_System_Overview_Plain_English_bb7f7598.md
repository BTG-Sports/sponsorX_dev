## 2. A quality score (who to recommend first)
SponsorX gives each athlete a score so staff know who to offer to a sponsor first. Importantly, follower count is NOT the main thing. What matters more:
- Engagement — how much their audience actually engages (likes, comments, shares)
- Quality — how good and professional their content is
- Reliability — do they deliver on time and follow instructions
- Location — are they in the right city/region for this sponsor

# Where BTG Fits In
In this first phase, BTG is the manager in the middle — we are not just handing people software and walking away. Our staff personally run the important steps:
- we recruit and approve the athletes
- we sell packages to businesses
- we match the right athletes to each campaign
- we check the content before it goes live
- we deliver the results report and ask for the renewal
The software's job right now is to organize and record all of that so nothing gets lost and every result can be proven. Later phases will automate more of it — but only after we have proven the loop works by hand.

# Quick FAQ
Q:  Is this a mobile game or an app you download?
A:  No. It is a website you log into. There is no game and nothing to install.
Q:  Are the athletes real?
A:  Yes — 100% real people with real social media accounts. We recruit and approve each one.
Q:  Do the 'cards' mean you collect athletes?
A:  No. Those are profile pages showing a real athlete and their prices, like a resume or menu. You hire them; you don't collect them.
Q:  Where does the money come from?
A:  Businesses pay for packages. We pay the athletes less than we charge the business; the difference is BTG's revenue.
Q:  What makes us different from just posting ads?
A:  We use real local athletes people trust, and we prove results with QR codes — scans, claims, and real customers who show up.
Q:  What is 'Phase 1' vs later phases?
A:  Phase 1 is this: real athletes, real campaigns, run partly by hand. Later phases add self-service buying, smarter pricing, and a virtual world (INFINEX). We build those only after Phase 1 works.
