## 1. Scope of Services
BTG will coordinate the approved SponsorX campaign described in Exhibit A, including athlete outreach/selection, campaign briefing, deliverable coordination, content approval workflow, tracking and campaign reporting to the extent included in the purchased package.
## 2. Sponsor Responsibilities
Sponsor will provide accurate brand/product information, approved logos and creative, campaign offers/reward terms, timely approvals, required products or access, and payment according to the agreed schedule. Sponsor is responsible for substantiating claims it asks athletes or BTG to communicate.
## 3. Athlete Participation
Athlete participation remains subject to athlete acceptance, eligibility, availability, applicable school/team/association rules, conflicts and required guardian/representative authorization. Unless a specific athlete is expressly guaranteed in writing, BTG may propose a reasonably comparable substitute if an athlete cannot participate.
## 4. Campaign Deliverables
The number and type of athletes, posts, reels, stories, appearances, media placements, rewards and other deliverables are limited to those expressly stated in Exhibit A or a signed amendment. Additional work may require additional fees.
## 5. Content Approval
Sponsor will provide approvals or requested revisions within the approval period stated in Exhibit A. Delayed approvals may require adjustment of publication dates. BTG may reject requested changes that conflict with athlete agreements, applicable law, platform rules, campaign disclosures or the approved scope.
## 6. Usage Rights
Sponsor receives only the content and name/image/likeness usage rights expressly purchased and documented in Exhibit A and applicable athlete Campaign Orders/releases. Ownership of athlete-created original content remains with the athlete unless expressly transferred.
## 7. Paid Media / Expanded Rights
Organic reposting rights do not automatically include paid advertising, boosted posts, whitelisting, television, print, billboards, packaging, perpetual use, sublicensing, AI training or synthetic recreation. Any expanded rights must be expressly identified, approved and, where applicable, separately compensated.
## 8. Exclusivity
Any sponsor category exclusivity applies only to the category, athletes, territory and period expressly stated in Exhibit A. Sponsor receives no general exclusivity by implication.
## 9. Fees & Payment
Sponsor will pay the Campaign Fee according to the Payment Schedule above. BTG may delay campaign activation or publication if required payments are not received when due. Taxes, third-party production, travel, product, paid media or other costs are included only if expressly stated.
## 10. Performance & Reporting
BTG will provide available campaign reporting based on the purchased package. Metrics may include views, reach, engagement, clicks, QR scans, claims and redemptions. BTG will distinguish verified, manually verified, self-reported, estimated or attributed metrics where practical.
## 11. No Sales Guarantee
Unless expressly guaranteed in a signed writing, BTG does not guarantee sales, revenue, leads, follower growth, impressions, redemptions or other commercial outcomes. Campaign results depend on athlete performance, platform distribution, audience response, sponsor offer quality and other factors outside BTG’s control.
## 12. Rewards / Offers
If Sponsor provides a discount, reward, coupon or promotion, Sponsor is responsible for honoring the approved offer according to the stated eligibility, locations, dates and restrictions. Sponsor will promptly inform BTG of exclusions or operational limitations.
## 13. Advertising Disclosures & Compliance
BTG will instruct participating athletes regarding required campaign disclosures. Sponsor will not request misleading endorsements, undisclosed advertising, unsubstantiated claims or activities prohibited by applicable law, school/team rules or platform policies.

# Pilot Creative Concept
Participating athletes create authentic vertical content around their personal pregame meal or food routine. The athlete should naturally complete the statement “Before a game, I gotta have _____,” show or discuss the meal, and close by asking viewers “What’s yours?” Sponsor integration should feel native to the athlete’s routine and follow the approved creative brief.
