# BTG SponsorX Athlete Network Program
## 1. Program Concept
SponsorX Athlete Network is BTG Sports Group's curated network of athletes available for paid brand content, appearances, promotions and community activations.
The operating model:
Brands pay SponsorX → SponsorX builds campaign → athletes opt in → athletes create/activate → BTG verifies deliverables → athletes are paid → sponsor receives performance report.
An athlete can participate as either:
BTG Represented Athlete — full BTG relationship, with SponsorX becoming one monetization channel.
SponsorX Content Partner — participates only in individual SponsorX opportunities under a limited content-collaboration agreement.
This second category is the key to scaling quickly.

# 9. Content Collaboration Agreement
The agreement should deliberately not turn every SponsorX athlete into a BTG representation client.
Its purpose is narrower.
### Core structure
Parties
BTG Sports Group LLC / SponsorX
and
Athlete.
### Relationship
Athlete joins the SponsorX Athlete Network as an independent content collaborator.
Participation does not automatically establish exclusive athlete representation.
### Opportunities
BTG may present paid sponsorship opportunities.
Athlete is free to accept or reject unless otherwise agreed for a particular campaign.
### Campaign Order
Every accepted campaign gets a short Campaign Order stating:
Sponsor
Deliverables
Deadline
Compensation
Usage rights
Exclusivity
Required disclosures
Approval requirements
This avoids rewriting the master agreement every campaign.
### Compensation
Athlete receives the compensation specified in the Campaign Order after completion and acceptance of required deliverables, subject to clearly disclosed payment timing and legitimate adjustments/refunds specified in the agreement.
### Content Rights
Athlete retains ownership of original athlete-created content unless otherwise agreed.
Athlete grants campaign-specific rights to:
BTG
Sponsor
iMC
for the period and channels specified.
Paid advertising usage should cost more.
Don't give sponsors perpetual unlimited rights by default.
### Athlete Responsibilities
Accurate information
Professional conduct
Meet deadlines
Follow campaign brief
Make legally required sponsorship disclosures
No fake engagement
No unauthorized sponsor claims
Notify BTG of conflicts
### BTG Responsibilities
Provide campaign brief
Communicate compensation
Coordinate approvals
Track deliverables
Provide campaign reporting
Process payment according to agreement
### Exclusivity
Only campaign-specific.
Example:
Athlete will not promote another sports drink for 30 days.
The athlete should be paid more for meaningful exclusivity.
### Termination
Either party can leave the network subject to already accepted campaigns and outstanding obligations.
### Independent Contractor
No employment relationship created.
### NIL/Eligibility
Athlete remains responsible for complying with applicable school, conference, association and jurisdiction requirements, with BTG also establishing its own compliance checks before campaign activation.
I would have Maryland sports/NIL counsel review the final contract before you deploy it, especially if high-school athletes/minors are included.

That structure lets SponsorX become much larger than BTG's own social following—which is exactly what you need at this stage.
